A musician first

DJ Lumi isn’t just someone who plays music at events. He’s a musician first, a former professional wind and saxophone player who has been performing live since 2014. That background changes everything. Where most DJs hear tracks, Lumi hears structure, tension, and release. Where others follow a playlist, he reads the room.

Born and raised in a culture where music is inseparable from celebration, Luis brings a bilingual fluency that goes beyond just speaking the language. Salsa dura, bachata, reggaeton, merengue, hip-hop, R&B — it all lives in the same set because it all lives in the same community. Southwest Florida’s Latin roots aren’t an afterthought here. They’re the foundation.

A live sound engineering background sharpens every performance on the technical side: clean transitions, balanced levels, and a floor that stays engaged because the energy is always controlled, never accidental. From an intimate wedding ceremony at a Naples venue to a high-energy quinceañera or corporate event, the standard doesn’t change.

The goal is simple

Create moments people are still talking about when the night is over.
